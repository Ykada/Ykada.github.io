The Creature Within

School project for Neon Orgines

Overview

Systems built for the experience

The Creature Within was a Unity school project made for Neon Orgines. We worked on it as a large team and came up with many different ideas and gameplay features. However, we did not have enough artists to turn all of those ideas into a finished game, so development stopped after around six months. Even though the project was not fully completed, I learned a lot from working on it. I worked on different gameplay and optimisation systems, including enemy AI, microphone input, automatic doors, and culling optimisation.
