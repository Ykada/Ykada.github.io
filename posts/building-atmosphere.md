# Building Atmosphere

Sometimes the smallest details do the most work: a distant sound, a light that flickers just once, or a corridor that feels a little too quiet.

## Notes to explore

- Give players room to notice the world.
- Let sound and lighting hint at what comes next.
- Keep the interface out of the way when the scene needs to breathe.
